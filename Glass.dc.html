Senior UX / Product Design Lead · 12+ yrs

Designing clarity at scale.

I'm a senior UX / Product Design leader with 12+ years across B2C and B2B, designing data-dense UIs, role-based workflows and multi-persona journeys for CRM, analytics and digital commerce. I own the problem from discovery to shipped design — research, IA, interaction and high-fidelity UI — and I'm known as a strong collaborator and team player who drives toward shared org goals and OKRs alongside Product, Engineering and Business.
My design decisions are insight-led and evidence-based, and I champion WCAG-compliant, inclusive systems, design-system governance, mentorship and Agile delivery. I also lead on applied AI in design — organising internal AI & design showcases to raise the wider team's craft. CXA & CUA certified (Human Factors International); MSc, Media & Communication Design, Anna University.
